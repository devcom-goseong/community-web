Why this community exists

Organised enough that everyone knows what is happening, open enough that anyone can walk in.

Plenty of students at KDU are teaching themselves to code, sketching product ideas, or quietly working on something interesting on their own. What was missing was an obvious place to find each other. This is that place.

The community is run by students, for students. A founding team is putting the rules, the platforms and the first activities in place so that anyone who joins knows what the community is, what to expect from it, what is expected from them, and who to ask when they need help.

We would rather be small and useful than large and noisy. Activities start at a size we can genuinely run, and grow as more people step forward to take responsibility for them.

Four things we do for each other

Learn together

A weekly meeting with one topic, plus member-run study groups for programming, data science, web, mobile and certification prep. Asking a basic question is normal here.

Build real things

Submit an idea, find people whose skills complete yours, and ship something that exists outside a tutorial. Teams need designers and writers as much as they need engineers.

Meet people

One in-person meetup every month for workshops, hands-on sessions and plain conversation, alongside guest speakers invited into the weekly discussion.

Show your work

Demo days give you a room, an audience and honest feedback — practice at presenting, a portfolio piece, and often a collaborator you did not know you needed.

What a term looks like

Not everything starts at once. We begin with the weekly meeting and the study groups, and add the rest as the community can carry them.

Not only for programmers

One project usually needs five different kinds of person. If only one of them shows up, the project does not get made.

  • Someone who writes the code
  • Someone who designs how it looks and behaves
  • Someone who understands AI or data
  • Someone who keeps the documentation honest
  • Someone who handles outreach and the business side
  • Someone who keeps everyone organised

You do not have to be a KDU student, and you do not have to be technical. Complete beginners are welcome, and so is anyone already working who wants people to build with. If you are not sure where you fit, say exactly that on the form — it is a normal answer, and we will help you find a starting point.

Come and build something with us.

Tell us who you are and what you are interested in. We read every message, and you will hear back from a person on the founding team — not an autoresponder.
