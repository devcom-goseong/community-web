What it is

Building something under a deadline, usually over a day or a weekend, usually with people you have just met. It is intense, slightly chaotic, and one of the fastest ways to find out what you can actually do.

What we take part in

  • Internal KDU hackathons
  • External hackathons
  • Online challenges
  • University competitions
  • Technology competitions
  • Industry-run challenges

Participation first, winning later

At the beginning the point is to take part and learn how these events work. Teams that have never entered one before do not win them, and treating the first few as practice is the honest way to approach it. As the community gets more experienced we will run larger events ourselves.

If you have never done one

  • You do not need to be good yet. Every team needs someone to test things, write the README, and prepare the presentation.
  • Scope down hard. The teams that finish build one small thing properly.
  • Sleep. The all-nighter is a myth that produces broken demos.
  • Prepare the presentation before you think you need to. A working project nobody understood scores worse than a modest one explained clearly.

How teams are chosen

We announce an event in the channel, people say whether they want in, and teams form around who is available. Nobody is picked or left out by the leadership team.
