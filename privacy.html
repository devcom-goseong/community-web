Privacy notice

What the KDU Developer Community collects when you use the form, what happens to it, who can see it, and how to have it deleted.

Version 1 Last reviewed 29 August 2026

This covers this website and the form on it. It is written to be read rather than to protect us. If anything here is unclear, ask, and we will fix the wording.

1. What we collect

Only what you type into the form:

  • Whether you are applying to join or asking a question
  • Your name and your email address
  • Whether you are a KDU student, and your student ID if you choose to give it
  • The areas of interest you tick
  • Your message
  • That you agreed to be contacted, and that you accepted the rules, the terms and this notice

Nothing else. There is no account to create, no profile, and nothing at all is collected as you read the site.

2. What happens to it

When you send the form it goes to a small program on our host, which turns it into two emails: a confirmation to you, so you know it arrived, and a notification to the community inbox. It is then stored in that inbox.

There is no database. This website does not have one, and your details are not written anywhere except those two emails.

3. Who can see it

The members of the leadership team who have access to the community inbox. Nobody outside the community.

4. What happens automatically

  • Your IP address is held in memory for a few minutes when you submit the form, to stop one source flooding it. It is not stored, and it is not put in either email.
  • Our host, Netlify, keeps standard server logs, as every web host does.
  • The emails are sent through Gmail, so Google handles them as it handles any email.
  • The typeface loads from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page opens. This is the only third party the pages contact.

5. What we do not do

  • No analytics, and no tracking of any kind.
  • This site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.
  • No advertising, and no marketing lists.
  • Your details are never sold, shared, or passed to anyone outside the leadership team.

6. How long we keep it

Your application stays in the community inbox for as long as you are a member. If we cannot accept your application we delete it once we have told you. If you ask us to delete it at any point, we delete it.

7. Asking for your data

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Reply to the confirmation email you received, or use the form and say what you want. We will do it and tell you when it is done.

8. Changes

If this notice changes we update the date at the top of this page, and announce anything material on the community platform.
