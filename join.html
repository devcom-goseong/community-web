Tell us who you are

One form does both jobs: apply for membership, or ask a question before you decide. Either way it reaches the same inbox and a person on the founding team reads it.

After you send it

  • A confirmation email arrives straight away, so you know it went through.
  • Someone on the founding team reads what you wrote.
  • If you are applying, we reply with an invitation to the community platform.
  • If you asked a question, we answer it.

What we do with your details

Your message goes to the community inbox and nowhere else. No analytics, no tracking, no marketing lists, nothing passed to anyone outside the founding team. Ask us to delete it and we will.

Complete beginners and non-technical members are welcome. If you are not sure where you fit, write exactly that — it is a normal answer.
